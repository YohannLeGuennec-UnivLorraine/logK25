# Rights statement

No single licence currently applies to the entire logK25 repository.

- **Original logK25 source code:** licence and institutional ownership are under review.
- **Consolidated dataset:** composed of transformed contributions subject to different source-specific conditions. Rights follow the underlying scientific dataset, not the software or format used to distribute it.
- **Third-party source files:** remain subject to the rights of their respective owners and providers.
- **Documentation and interface content:** licence under institutional review.

The absence of a repository-wide licence does not place the repository or its contents in the public domain and does not grant unrestricted reuse rights.

Some contributions have documented reuse permission, including AqSolDB data (CC0), IUPAC pKa data for non-commercial use (CC BY-NC 4.0), SOLTHERM (CC BY 4.0), JESS under its custom permission, and the NIST SRD 46 SQL-derived data under the Fair Use terms attached to that distribution. Other contributions remain restricted or under review at file level. In particular, a GWB or Medusa/Spana label identifies a technical distribution route and must not be interpreted as a common licence.

See [SOURCES_AND_LICENSES.md](SOURCES_AND_LICENSES.md) for the source-by-source register, attribution requirements and known reuse conditions.
